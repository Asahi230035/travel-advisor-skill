# Traveler profiles, group coordination, and cross-border readiness

Load this playbook when the party includes children, older adults, accessibility needs, multi-preference groups, solo travelers, or international/cross-border travel.

## 1. Traveler profile adaptations (apply only what matches the party)

### Traveling with children (亲子游)
- **Pacing & naps**: for toddlers/young kids, preserve a midday rest window (or base hotel within 15–20 min transit for an afternoon reset). Limit long continuous indoor museum blocks.
- **Logistics**: check stroller accessibility (stairs-only historic sites, narrow metro gates, cobblestones), child car-seat rules for taxis/rentals, and luggage volume.
- **Tickets & meals**: verify age/height cutoffs for free/child tickets (live-check — thresholds vary widely by country and operator); keep mild/non-spicy meal options nearby when visiting spicy-food cities.

### Traveling with seniors or mobility constraints (带长辈 / 无障碍)
- **Step & slope discipline**: respect stated step limits; avoid steep hillside temples, multi-transfer underground corridors without elevators, or long standing queues without seating.
- **Daily rhythm**: start comfortably (not pre-dawn), include seated tea/coffee/rest breaks every 1.5–2 hours, avoid late-night dining, and prefer door-to-door taxis/ride-hailing over complex multi-transfer transit when budget allows.
- **Hotel fit**: prioritize elevator access, walk-in shower safety, quiet rooms, and short flat walks to breakfast/transit.

### Small groups with mixed interests (多人小团分合机制)
When travelers have diverging priorities (e.g., museums vs shopping vs photography vs resting):
- **Core shared anchors**: align everyone on 1–2 anchor experiences per day + shared meals (especially dinner).
- **Planned split-and-rejoin windows (2–3 hours)**: design natural parallel tracks *in the same neighborhood* (e.g., Group A visits the museum while Group B explores the adjacent shopping street or rests at a cafe), with a **fixed rejoin time and easy landmark/restaurant**.
- **Never force 100% togetherness** at the expense of exhausting half the group.

### Solo travelers (独行)
- Check **single-room supplements** (especially ryokans/resorts priced per person) and solo-diner friendliness (some hotpot/course restaurants require 2+ diners or charge extra).
- Emphasize well-lit, transit-connected evening neighborhoods and practical safety habits without alarmism.

## 2. Cross-border & international travel essentials

For international trips, **proactively check and surface** relevant hard blockers — always citing official live sources (`✅`) or flagging `❓` for official confirmation:
- **Entry, visa & transit rules**: visa requirement, visa-free stay duration, e-visa/ETA/electronic arrival card deadlines (e.g., Visit Japan Web, K-ETA, ESTA, ETIAS, SG Arrival Card), onward-ticket/accommodation proof. **Always note that official immigration/embassy rules govern.**
- **Passport validity**: flag the common **6-month remaining validity** rule (and blank page requirements) whenever travel dates are near.
- **Payments & cash**: card acceptance vs cash-only pockets (e.g., small shrines, rural buses, coin lockers, street stalls), mobile wallet setup, IC/transit cards (e.g., Suica/ICOCA/T-money/Oyster/contactless), tipping norms, and VAT/tax-free refund thresholds.
- **Connectivity & power**: eSIM / roaming / pocket Wi-Fi needs, local map/ride-hailing/translation apps worth installing *before* departure, plug/voltage differences.
- **Reservation culture & etiquette**: venues requiring advance time-slot reservations, luggage-forwarding services (e.g., takkyubin in Japan), quiet-car/dining etiquette, or dress codes for religious sites.

## 3. On-demand pre-departure checklist

**Do not dump a generic packing list unprompted.** Deliver a concise, trip-tailored pre-departure checklist **only when the user asks** (or as a compact 6–8 bullet closing section for an explicit full international trip plan):
- **Documents & Entry**: passport validity, visa/ETA, flight/hotel confirmations, travel insurance (medical + delay/cancellation coverage).
- **Bookings & Apps**: time-critical reservations from the booking countdown, offline maps, transit IC/app, ride-hailing app.
- **Money & Connectivity**: backup payment card, small local cash, eSIM/roaming activated before landing.
- **Season & Profile Specifics**: weather gear matched to actual forecast/season, adapters, regular medications (note restricted-medicine checks for destinations like Japan/UAE/Singapore), kid/senior comfort items.
